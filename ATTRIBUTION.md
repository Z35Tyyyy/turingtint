# Attribution and scope

No repository-wide application license has been supplied. Existing source
copyright notices are retained; this deployment does not grant a new license
to application code. Third-party components retain their own licenses.

This CPU deployment includes no model weights or raw training corpus. The
retrieval library contains twelve public Europe PMC / PLOS ONE abstracts under
[CC-BY-4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Authors, original links,
license evidence and abstract-extraction information remain in each database
record and are listed below. This is a demonstration collection, not
internet-wide source coverage.

[HC3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Hello-SimpleAI/HC3) is credited to Guo et al.,
*How Close is ChatGPT to Human Experts? Comparison Corpus, Evaluation, and
Detection* (2023). Its declared CC-BY-SA-4.0 terms and Wikipedia origins apply
to the included HC3 demo excerpts and concatenated example, which remain under
[CC-BY-SA-4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Other demo passages retain their original provenance and licenses below.
Synthetic project exercises are identified as synthetic, not evidence of
real student authorship. No authorship capability is approved.
